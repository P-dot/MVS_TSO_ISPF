Lab 05 — ISPF Browse Navigation, Scrolling, LOCATE and Labels

Architecture V2:
Domain: Operations and Service Management
Capability: ISPF read-only data inspection and navigation
Lifecycle: Discover / Operate
Maturity: M1 — Foundational
Integration: I0 — Standalone
Validation: VALIDATED

Interactive sequence:

1. Open DSLIST
   =3.4

2. Locate/select data set
   IBMUSER.JCL.LAB
   B

3. Select member
   S  L10PREP

4. Browse baseline
   BROWSE IBMUSER.JCL.LAB(L10PREP)
   Scroll initially observed as CSR

5. Page scrolling
   Scroll ===> PAGE
   PF8 / Down

6. Half-page scrolling
   Scroll ===> HALF
   PF8 / Down

7. Numeric scrolling
   Scroll ===> 10
   PF8 / Down

8. Maximum scroll to bottom
   Scroll ===> MAX
   PF8 / Down

Observed:
   Bottom of Data
   Scroll field subsequently returned to 0010

9. Maximum scroll to top
   Scroll ===> MAX
   PF7 / Up

Observed:
   Top of Data

10. Locate line 40
    L 40

11. Assign label
    .MARK

Observed:
    Label '.MARK' assigned

12. Move away from saved position
    BOTTOM
    (or equivalent movement to the lower boundary)

13. Locate label
    L MARK

Observed:
    Label 'MARK' located

14. Terminate Browse
    PF3 / END

Observed:
    Return to IBMUSER.JCL.LAB member list

Persistent z/OS changes:
NONE

Batch RC:
NOT APPLICABLE
